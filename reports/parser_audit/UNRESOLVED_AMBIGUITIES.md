# Remaining source and scientific ambiguities

These are not confirmed parser implementation bugs. They restrict interpretation even though the transformations reconcile.

|---|---|---|---|
| U01 | 13,485 retained VTD records precede commissioning; 14,239 local-wall/nominal disagreements | Pre-commissioning targets excluded. Derived pit_corrosion_intensity, passport diagnostic conclusions, and archival gravimetric fields are not permitted preprocessing model features. The legacy ML thermal feature registry includes modeling defaults and assumptions; this audit does not certify their physical accuracy or tune them. Existing temperature stages and Ea decisions were not changed.
